How the ranking works

implied value = TVL × multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Multiple — the median market-cap-to-TVL of the snapshot's own live-token protocols in the same category (at least 3 of them); otherwise the median across every live-token protocol. Clamped to 0.05 – 1.50.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7-day change, −40%, +80%) × 0.4.
  • Maturity — 0.70 + 0.30 × min(age, 1095 days) / 1095. Unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Breadth — 1 + 0.02 × min(chains − 1, 4).

No protocol name is ever special-cased: the same function scores every row. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are kept out, as are exchange products and versioned names — they will not launch a token of their own.

Implied value is a model, not a promise: it says what a launch would be worth if the protocol priced like the live-token protocols in its category. "Pre-token" means the snapshot records no token — not that a token will ever exist.
